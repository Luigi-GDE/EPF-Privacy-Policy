Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Extreme Pomodoro Fishing ("we", "us", or "the game") handles information when you play the game.

1. Information Stored On Your Device

The game currently does not require an account and does not send your gameplay data to us or to any server we control. Instead, the following is saved locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Save game progress (up to 3 save slots), including unlocked items, in-game currency, and catch history
  • Quest and objective progress
  • Audio, music, and ambiance volume settings
  • Whether you have accepted this Privacy Policy

This local data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the game or clear its app data/storage.

2. Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads powered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) so you can earn in-game rewards. To do this:

  • Before ads load, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent where required (e.g. under GDPR or UK rules) and to determine whether ads are personalized or non-personalized.
  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect information such as advertising/device identifiers, general (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, app usage, and interactions with ads, in order to select, serve, and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • We do not receive or store the raw advertising data collected by AdMob ourselves - it is processed by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies.
  • On iOS, ad tracking additionally requires your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

You can manage ad personalization through your device's ad settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, or "Limit Ad Tracking"/App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

3. No Accounts, Cloud Saves, or Purchases Today

As of this version, the game does not require sign-in, does not upload your save data to a cloud account, and does not process any real-money purchases. If a future update adds features such as cloud saves, sign-in, leaderboards, or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated to describe what additional data is collected and you will be asked to review and re-accept it before continuing.

4. Children's Privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because gameplay data is stored only on-device, we do not otherwise collect personal information through normal use of the game.

5. Data Security

Locally stored data relies on the security protections provided by your device's operating system. No method of electronic storage or transmission (including by third-party ad services) is completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You can delete your locally stored save data and settings at any time by clearing the app's data/storage through your device settings, or by uninstalling the game. You can adjust ad personalization through your device or ad-network settings as described above.

7. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game changes, for example if new features begin collecting additional data. If we make material changes, we will ask you to review and re-accept the updated policy the next time you launch the game.
